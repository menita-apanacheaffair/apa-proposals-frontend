01 — ABOUT

About A Panache Affair

A Panache Affair is a bespoke event planning company, dedicated to crafting exquisite and personalized experiences for each client. Our approach is rooted in understanding your unique needs and desires, ensuring that every detail reflects your vision.

Behind the scenes, our team of professionals thoughtfully choreograph each element of your event, weaving together logistics, design, and guest experience into one cohesive whole. You are free to be fully present in the moment while we quietly handle the moving pieces and ensure your celebration feels effortless, polished, and unforgettable.

Meet the Visionary

Shveta Dhillon, Founder & CEO

My name is Michelle Shveta Dhillon, CEO of A Panache Affair. With over 13 years of successful event planning experience and 21 years in the hospitality and sales industry, I remain passionate about creating exceptional experiences for my clients.

My strong background in identifying client needs led me to establish A Panache Affair and the APA Standard, which centers on crafting customized events that reflect the unique personalities and visions of our clients. We believe in building personal relationships with our clients, understanding their vision, desires and earning their trust. The goal is to go above expectations, and execute perfection.

Our focus is not on the quantity of weddings we plan, but on the quality and attention to detail we bring to each event. We take pride in making our clients' visions a reality.

This is the APA Standard. This is A Panache Affair.
